ABOUT SMARTDRIVE

A simpler way to find your next ride.

SmartDrive is a vehicle-rental platform for exploring cars, motorcycles, and recreational vehicles in the Philippines. Browse available rides, compare vehicle details, and choose dates that fit your plans.

From discovering a vehicle to reviewing booking details, SmartDrive brings the rental journey together in one place. Availability, rental requirements, and final booking terms are shown as part of the booking process.

OUR STORY · 2026

From a small rental app to a smarter way to get moving.

SmartDrive began in 2026 as a small vehicle-rental app idea. The goal was to bring vehicle discovery and the steps of arranging a rental into one straightforward digital experience.

It started with the essentials: browse vehicles, choose rental dates, and move through a booking. The project has continued to evolve with vehicle details, saved favorites, account tools, payment options, and clearer booking information.

HOW IT WAS MADE

Built and improved as a team.

SmartDrive was developed as a student team project at the Technological Institute of the Philippines – Quezon City Campus. The work brought together planning and design, the customer-facing web and mobile experience, backend services, APIs, and database development.

The app uses Angular and Ionic for its interface, with Supabase supporting data and account services. Capacitor helps bring the web experience to Android.
