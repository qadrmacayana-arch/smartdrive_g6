OUR STORY · 2026

From a small rental app to a smarter way to get moving.

SmartDrive began in 2026 as a small vehicle-rental app idea. The goal was to bring vehicle discovery and the steps of arranging a rental into one straightforward digital experience.

It started with the essentials: browse vehicles, choose rental dates, and move through a booking. The project has since evolved with features shaped around the renter, including vehicle details, saved favorites, account tools, payment options, and clearer booking information.

HOW IT WAS MADE

Built and improved as a team.

SmartDrive was developed as a student team project at the Technological Institute of the Philippines – Quezon City Campus. The work brought together planning and design, the customer-facing web and mobile experience, backend services, APIs, and database development.

The app uses Angular and Ionic for its interface, with Supabase supporting data and account services. Capacitor helps bring the web experience to Android. The team continues to refine the project as its features grow.
