Read rental requirements and guidelines

Driver eligibility and documents

Self-drive rentals commonly require drivers to be 20–21 or older; some luxury or heavy SUV vehicles may require age 25+. Staff will verify the vehicle-specific age requirement and original documents before handover.

Local renters should bring a valid Philippine Non-Professional or Professional Driver’s License, two government-issued photo IDs, and proof of billing. Foreign tourists should bring a passport with Philippine entry stamp and a valid license. An English-language foreign license is generally accepted for up to 90 days after arrival; a license in a non-Roman alphabet may require an IDP or official embassy translation.

Rental duration and timing

Rates are generally based on 24-hour periods. Standard rentals may require a 1–2 day minimum and peak holiday periods may require at least 3 days. Late returns may incur ₱300–₱500 per hour; delays over 3 hours may be charged as an additional day. Early returns are generally not prorated.

Where and how you may drive

Ask SmartDrive for written approval before taking a vehicle on an inter-island RoRo ferry. Driving on beaches, flooded roads, or unpaved trails is prohibited. Chauffeur service, if available, must be arranged with SmartDrive in advance.

Follow local number-coding rules. In areas applying the weekday schedule, plate endings 1–2 are coded Monday, 3–4 Tuesday, 5–6 Wednesday, 7–8 Thursday, and 9–0 Friday, commonly 7:00 AM–8:00 PM. Local rules and exemptions can vary.

Tolls, fuel, and mileage

Renters are responsible for tolls and for topping up or reimbursing Autosweep/Easytrip RFID use. Vehicles are generally returned with the same fuel level received. Refueling may incur pump cost plus a service charge; commonly cited charges range from ₱400–₱500 or a markup up to 15%. Some rentals may limit mileage to 100–200 km per day and charge ₱12–₱25 per excess kilometer.

Deposits and cancellations

Local operators commonly request a refundable security deposit of ₱3,000–₱10,000. Some require a credit card in the primary driver’s name. Cancellation and no-show refunds depend on the confirmed booking terms; short-notice cancellations or no-shows may forfeit a reservation deposit. Contact SmartDrive before paying if you need the exact deposit, payment, cancellation, or refund terms.

These amounts and practices are general estimates, not a SmartDrive price quote or a promise that a particular charge applies.
